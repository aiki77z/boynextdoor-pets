# Catbbi / 猫比 Hatch Prompt

## Base Pet

Original fan-made 2D desktop pet inspired by a white cat impression associated with Sungho, but not a portrait and not a copy of any official character art. Keep it non-realistic and non-3D. Do not use pixel-art rendering, clay rendering, plush photo rendering, realistic fur, or glossy figurine shading. Gentle simple shading is allowed if it helps keep the same soft mascot feeling as the main reference.

Catbbi is a milk-white chibi cat with a very large head and a tiny body. The silhouette must stay compact and readable at desktop-pet scale. The head is the main identity anchor: broad rounded-square face, sharp pointed cat ears, orange caps on the upper ear tips, and a small orange patch centered between the ears on top of the forehead. The face has soft asymmetric beige patches: a larger pale beige patch around the left eye area and a softer beige patch on the lower right cheek. Eyes are large black ovals with visible white rims, slightly tilted and not perfectly symmetrical. The left eye must not collapse into a plain black dot. Tiny black nose, small simple cat mouth, and very long thin whiskers that extend horizontally outward with a slight downward curve, especially on the lower whiskers. Expression should feel quiet, sensitive, sweet, and a little bit superstar-like. The overall finish should feel like a clean 2D mascot character sheet, not a pixel sprite and not a rendered toy.

The body is very small compared with the head. Arms are short beige stubs. Feet are small, flat, and beige. Chest has a small orange clover-like flower mark that feels original and simple. Tail should stay subtle and mostly hidden in front-facing poses. Overall feel: sensitive white cat, sweet but reserved, with a shy fitness contrast.

## Action Mapping For Hatch Pet

Because the hatch-pet atlas uses standard rows, map Catbbi's custom motions into the following standard animation ideas:

- idle: gentle blink, tiny breathing motion, slight ear twitch
- waving: shy greeting with one paw, small reserved smile
- jumping: happy little hop with bright eyes
- sleeping: curled into a compact cat ball, tail wrapped close
- review: shy blink, then a tiny fitness pose with paws close to chest
- failed: mini push-up attempt or tiny dumbbell strain, then bashful smile
- running-right: tiny careful walking cycle toward the right, balanced and neat
- running-left: same gait toward the left, mirrored only if visual identity stays correct

## Pixel Style Rules

Use the existing Catbbi reference proportions as source truth: front-facing cat, big head, tiny body, clean readable outline, flat soft colors, and very restrained simple shading. Prioritize these recognisable anchors in every row:

- pointed ears with orange tips
- orange forehead patch
- asymmetric beige face patches
- large black eyes with white rims
- long fine whiskers
- orange chest clover mark
- tiny beige arms and flat beige feet

Keep every frame clean and transparent-ready. No text, no floating symbols, no detached sparkles, no background scene, no cast shadows, no floor shadows, no realistic anatomy, no pixel-art conversion, and no human likeness.

## Recommended Stable Palette

- milk white body
- soft beige face patches and limbs
- warm orange ear tips, forehead patch, and chest mark
- black outline and facial features
- white eye rims

## Notes For Consistency

Catbbi should always read as a gentle white cat first, not a generic mascot. The head shape, ear shape, eye treatment, whisker length, and small orange chest mark are the minimum identity anchors that cannot drift between rows. When in doubt, keep poses simpler and preserve identity.
